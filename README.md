# Rangpur-LULC-Assessment-2026
Spatial analysis and Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) classification of Rangpur District, Bangladesh (October 2026) using Landsat multispectral imagery and QGIS.

Project Description

This project presents a spatial Land Use and Land Cover (LULC) assessment of Rangpur District, Bangladesh, derived from Landsat multispectral satellite imagery acquired on 16 October 2026. Using QGIS, the workflow classifies and quantifies four major land cover classes across a total analyzed area of 2,363.06 sq. km. Through supervised classification and spatial area calculation, the study maps agricultural, urban, hydrological, and vegetation distributions, producing statistical summaries and a publication-ready cartographic layout.

Key Highlights

Multispectral Image Processing: Processed Landsat satellite imagery (16 October 2026) in QGIS, establishing accurate spatial alignment within the 89.00°E–89.50°E and 25.50°N–26.00°N geographic extent.

Quantitative Land Cover Breakdown:

Quantified spatial coverage across four primary classes: Agriculture at 1,440.32 sq. km (60.95%), Urban/Built-up at 729.13 sq. km (30.86%), Water Bodies at 125.41 sq. km (5.31%), and Dense Vegetation at 68.20 sq. km (2.89%).

Spatial Pattern Evaluation:

 Identified continuous agricultural dominance across the district, dense urban clustering around Rangpur Sadar and major road corridors, and surface water concentrated along the Teesta River.

Cartographic Map Output:

 Generated a high-resolution thematic LULC map complete with coordinate grids, scale bar, north arrow, and class legend for spatial reporting.
